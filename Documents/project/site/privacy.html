Climby

Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Climby is a homework tutor for students in grades 1 to 12. It is made by one person, Martin Hristov, in Bulgaria. This page says what the app collects, what it deliberately does not keep, who else handles the data on the way, and how to have it removed. It is written to be read, not skimmed for the exit.

The short version

  • Photos of pages you send to the tutor are read once and discarded. The photo is not stored; the text read from it is, as part of your history.
  • What is stored: your account, your tasks, the text of your questions and the tutor's answers, and how long you studied.
  • Parents and teachers who are linked to a student see numbers only — never the text of a task or a question.
  • If a student sets up Crew, other students can find them by username and see their name and grade; friends also see their photo, holds, streak and whether they are studying right now.
  • The study camera never sends an image anywhere. It runs on your device and is off unless you turn it on. Starting a Focus session requires a brief face check; its temporary reference stays in memory only for that session.
  • You can download everything, or delete the account and everything in it, from Settings — immediately, no email needed.

What we collect, and why

Account. A display name, an email address, and a password (stored as a one-way hash, never in the clear). A username is optional. New password-based accounts must provide and verify a phone number by SMS as well as verify their email. Existing accounts without a phone can continue to sign in. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google email and name and nothing else; we do not see your Google password.

Questions and answers. When you ask the tutor something — by photo or in the chat — the text of your question and of the answer is saved to your history so you can look back at it, together with the text the AI read from the photographed page. The photo itself is not saved (see below).

ClimbAI memory. In Settings, a signed-in student can choose whether chat uses their profile grade, unfinished tasks, a short summary of up to seven recent focus sessions, a few earlier questions, and/or a note they write. Every category starts off. One thing is always sent: when the tutor reads a photographed page, your profile grade goes with it so the reading fits your level. The city in a profile is never included automatically, and ClimbAI does not infer personality or location. The note and category choices are stored in this browser on this device, not in the Climby account. If selected, the relevant context is sent with the chat request through Climby to Anthropic to answer that message; categories turned off are not included. The note is not saved as a separate server memory, but question and answer history continues to be saved as described above. Turns in the current chat are sent for continuity. A guest cannot use account memory, though their current chat still works.

Tasks. What you type into the Route: a task, a subject, a deadline, and whether it's done.

Study sessions. When you use the Ascent timer, we store how long the session lasted and, if the camera was on, what share of that time someone was at the desk — as a percentage. Nothing else about the session is recorded.

Class and family links. Which teacher's class you joined, and which parent is linked to you, so that they can see your numbers. Links are made with a code you hand over yourself, and a parent link can be removed by either side.

Crew (optional). Crew lets students study alongside friends. Setting it up stores your month and year of birth (the age rules need an age), a username, and, if you add one, a profile photo. Crew also stores your friends, friend requests, the people you blocked, the holds you earned for studying, and, while a focus session runs, the fact that it is running. Anyone with Crew can search by username and see a student's display name and grade; only friends — both of you said yes — see the photo, holds, streak and live status. Friends never see tasks, questions or answers. A block hides each of you from the other completely. Students under 12 can use Crew only after a linked parent allows it from their own account.

Online voices (optional). If you choose an online voice for reading answers aloud, the text being read is sent to Microsoft's Azure speech service to turn it into audio. The audio is kept briefly in the server's memory so the same answer does not have to be made twice. If you use the computer's own voice, nothing is sent.

Technical logs. Our server keeps short-lived logs that include your IP address, used to limit abuse (for example, how many requests an address can make per hour) and to diagnose failures. They are not used to profile anyone.

What we deliberately do not keep

Photos of pages. (This is about pages for the tutor, not the Crew profile photo above.) A photograph you send to the tutor — from the laptop camera, from your phone, from a file, or from a past exam paper — is sent to the AI provider to read the problem, and then discarded. It is not written to our database and does not appear in your history.

There is one exception, and it lasts seconds: a photo taken on a linked phone is held on our server until your computer collects it, usually within a second or two. If the computer never collects it, it is deleted after ten minutes. Collecting it deletes it.

Camera frames from the study timer. The Ascent timer can, if you switch it on, check whether someone is at the desk. That check happens entirely in the app on your device. No frame is uploaded, saved, or seen by anyone. The camera is off by default.

Required face check for Focus. Before a Focus session starts, Climby compares a few face descriptors on your device and keeps a temporary reference in memory only for that session. The check pauses the session only if it clearly sees a different face for several seconds in a row — not when no face is visible — and the session carries on by itself once it sees the original face again. The reference is erased when the session ends; it is never written to storage or sent to Climby or an AI provider. This is a continuity check, not liveness detection or secure identity authentication. The face-processing library and model are downloaded from jsDelivr; as with any web request, jsDelivr sees your IP address and request metadata, but not camera frames or face descriptors.

What parents and teachers see. A linked parent or teacher sees how many tasks you finished, how many are pending or overdue, how many minutes you studied this week, and how many days in a row. They never see the text of a task, a question, or an answer. This is a design decision, not a setting: a student who knows every question is being read stops asking honestly, and asking is the point.

Who else handles the data

Climby runs on services operated by other companies. Each one sees only what it needs to do its job:

  • Anthropic — the AI that reads your photo and writes the answer. Your photo and question are sent to Anthropic's API to generate the answer. Under Anthropic's API terms, that data is not used to train their models.
  • Render — hosts the server that runs Climby.
  • Neon — hosts the database where your account, tasks, history and sessions are stored.
  • Resend — sends the verification and password-reset emails. It sees your email address and the message.
  • Twilio — sends the SMS verification text required for new password-based accounts. It sees the number and the code.
  • Microsoft (Azure Speech) — only if you choose an online voice. It receives the text being read aloud.
  • Google — only if you choose to sign in with Google. This website also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address.
  • GitHub — hosts the app downloads and this page.
  • jsDelivr — delivers libraries the app loads while you use it: the face-processing library and model, the image tools for photographing pages, the maths renderer and the PDF viewer for exam papers. It receives each download request and your IP address, but never your photos, camera frames, questions or face descriptors.

Outside the EU. Anthropic, Resend and Twilio are based in the United States, and Microsoft and Google may process data there, so some data leaves the European Union. These transfers rely on each provider's standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

We do not sell data, show advertising, or share data with anyone for marketing. There is no analytics or tracking in the app.

Why we are allowed to

  • To provide the service you asked for (account, tutor, tasks, sessions, Crew, voices): performing our agreement with you.
  • Verification codes, rate limits and logs: our legitimate interest in keeping accounts and the service safe.
  • Optional extras (ClimbAI memory, the study camera, Crew for a younger student): your consent, or a parent's, which you can withdraw in Settings at any time.

On your device

The app keeps some things on your computer — language, theme, text size, whether the tour has been shown, your sign-in token if you chose "remember me", your ClimbAI chats, and a copy of your profile photo. These stay on the device and are not sent to us. This website sets no cookies.

Children

Climby is meant for students, many of whom are children. If you are under 14, a parent or guardian should create the account with you, or agree to you creating it. A parent can ask us at any time what is stored for their child's account and have it deleted — see below. We never show advertising to anyone, and we never share a child's data with anyone for any commercial purpose.

How long we keep things

For as long as the account exists. Verification and reset codes expire after 15 minutes. Phone photos are deleted when collected or after ten minutes. Server logs are kept for a short period for diagnostics and then discarded.

Your rights

In Settings you can change your password, download a copy of everything we hold about you as one file, and delete the account. Deletion is immediate and total: it removes the account, tasks, history, sessions, linked phones, Crew profile, photo, friends, requests, blocks and holds, and any parent or class links; a linked parent or teacher simply stops seeing your numbers. Nothing is kept in the app afterwards; copies in the hosting providers’ short-lived logs and backups expire on their own schedule. If you would rather we did it for you, or want something corrected, email martin.hr.original11@gmail.com from the address on the account and we will do it within seven days.

If you are in the European Union you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority; in Bulgaria that is the Commission for Personal Data Protection (КЗЛД).

Security

When using hosted Climby, connections to the server use HTTPS; remote PostgreSQL database connections use TLS. Passwords are hashed. Account data is not end-to-end encrypted, and the app does not add application-level encryption at rest; stored-data protection depends on the database provider. A phone linked to the app receives a key that can do exactly two things — upload a photo and ask whether it is still linked — and nothing else. A lost phone can send you a photo; it cannot read anything.
