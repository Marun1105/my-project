Terms of use

Last updated 29 October 2026

Climby is a free homework tutor for students in grades 1 to 12, made by one person, Martin Hristov, in Bulgaria. By creating an account you agree to these terms and to the privacy policy. They are short on purpose.

Who can use Climby

Anyone can use Climby as a student, parent or teacher. If you are under 14, a parent or guardian must agree to you having an account. When you tick the box at sign-up, you confirm that you are 14 or older, or that they agree. Crew, where students see each other, stays closed for students under 14 until a linked parent allows it.

What Climby is for

Climby helps you understand your homework. It is built to show the next step, not to hand in work for you. Use it to learn; what you hand in at school is your own responsibility.

Answers come from an AI

ClimbAI is an artificial intelligence. It is usually right and sometimes wrong — about a sum, a date or a rule of grammar. Check what matters with your textbook or your teacher. Climby is not a substitute for a teacher, and nothing it says is medical, legal or other professional advice.

Your account

  • Give your real name and your own email and phone number. One person, one account.
  • Keep your password to yourself. If you think someone else has it, change it in Settings.
  • Don't pretend to be someone else — in particular, don't create a parent account to approve Crew for yourself or for a friend.

Crew: be a good rope partner

  • Be kind. No bullying, threats, mocking or pressure — not through names, usernames, photos or cheers.
  • Your profile photo must be of you or something harmless. Nothing hurtful, sexual or violent, and no photos of other people without their permission.
  • Don't create fake accounts to send friend requests or to collect holds.
  • If someone bothers you, block them — they will not see you again — and tell a parent or teacher. You can also write to us at the address below.

Things you may not do

  • Try to get into someone else's account or see their data.
  • Overload the service, get round its limits, or use it to run automated requests.
  • Use Climby to break the law or school rules.

When we close an account

If an account breaks these rules, we may hide its Crew profile, remove a photo, or close the account. Where we can, we will say why first. You can always download your data before an account is closed, and you can delete your own account at any time in Settings.

No guarantees

Climby is free and provided as it is. We work to keep it running and your data safe, but we cannot promise it will always be available, error-free, or that every answer is correct. As far as the law allows, Climby is not responsible for losses that come from using it — such as a mark on homework that followed a wrong answer. Nothing here takes away rights that consumer law gives you.
